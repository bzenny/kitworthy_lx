# Kitworth.Studio Context

## Identity

Kitworth.Studio

**Digital value for the Real World.**

Kitworth is a digital product studio focused on turning validated needs into useful, market-ready digital products.

---

## Purpose of This Repository

This repository is the version-controlled authority layer for the Studio.

It exists so that humans and AI systems can quickly understand:

- what Kitworth is
- how products move through the Studio
- which information is authoritative
- which work is experimental
- what products are active
- why major decisions were made

---

## Authority Order

When information conflicts, use this order:

1. Explicit current product or business directives
2. Canonical files in this repository
3. Approved production artifacts
4. Development documentation
5. Research and reference material
6. AI-generated working material
7. Sandbox experiments

Nothing inside `_SANDBOX/` is authoritative.

---

## Core Repository Areas

### `_AI/`

Instructions and context designed to help AI systems work correctly inside Kitworth.

### `_INFOSTORE/`

Stable reference information about the brand, product system, markets, terminology, and operating assumptions.

### `_SANDBOX/`

Disposable experimentation.

Nothing here is canonical.

### `10_STRATEGY/`

Studio-level strategy, roadmap, business model, and direction.

### `20_PRODUCTS/`

Canonical records for products that have entered the Studio pipeline.

### `30_DEVELOPMENT/`

Active development work.

Product testing evidence belongs with the product here.

### `40_PRODUCTION/`

Approved release-ready and production materials.

### `50_ARCHIVE/`

Retired, superseded, or historical material.

---

## Product Flow

Products generally move through:

Opportunity → Validation → Definition → Development → Usability → Release → Market Learning → Scale / Revise / Retire

Movement between stages should be deliberate rather than automatic.

---

## Working Principle

Generate broadly.

Validate aggressively.

Develop deliberately.

Release professionally.

Learn from the market.
