# Kitworth AI Build Rules

AI systems working with Kitworth should follow these rules.

## 1. Preserve Authority

Do not overwrite canonical information simply because another interpretation appears plausible.

Flag contradictions.

## 2. Separate Fact From Proposal

Clearly distinguish:

- established information
- inference
- recommendation
- experiment

## 3. Stay Inside the Value Stream

Work should contribute toward:

Opportunity → Validation → Product → Release → Market Learning

Avoid unnecessary infrastructure.

## 4. Prefer Useful Over Impressive

Do not add complexity merely because a tool can generate it.

## 5. Promote Deliberately

Experimental material stays experimental until explicitly promoted.

## 6. Maintain Product Traceability

Important product decisions should be understandable later.

Record:

- what changed
- why
- evidence
- decision
- outcome

## 7. Protect Product Quality

Speed is valuable only when it does not materially reduce usability, accuracy, or trust.
